  • Window: selects signals by their original signal date. Outcomes always use all available price history after each signal.
  • 5D / 20D: the 5th and 20th trading session after the signal date. 1M: the first session on or after one calendar month later (about 21–23 sessions), so it is related to, but not the same as, 20D.
  • Completed periods only: a signal enters an average once its period has finished. Missing results are never treated as zero.
  • Direction: Buy-direction signals gain when the price rises; Sell-direction signals gain when it falls. Raw stock returns are kept alongside. These are not your realized P/L.
  • Overlap: signals on the same ticker and direction within 20 trading sessions are statistically dependent. They are flagged but never excluded.
